External Libraries, Observability Stack & Academic References

I. External Libraries: Functional Mapping

The +Ciclo Engine relies on a specialized stack of spatial and mathematical libraries. Each function is mapped to a specific internal module to ensure maintenance locality.

Library Key Functions Internal Module Role in Simulation
GDAL/OGR ogr2ogr infra/ingestion.py High-performance C-level streaming of metropolitan datasets.
Pandas groupby(), merge() core/demand_synthesizer.py Calculation of gravitational weights for disaggregation.
GeoPandas to_crs(), sjoin() infra/ingestion.py, core/routing.py Coordinate alignment and KNN snapping of demand to graph.
OSMnx graph_from_place() infra/ingestion.py Automated retrieval of topological road networks from OSM.
H3 (Uber) polyfill(), h3_to_coords() core/demand_synthesizer.py Hexagonal hierarchical indexing for demand disaggregation.
Shapely ST_Split, ST_Snap core/network_refactor.py Virtual geometric operations (mimicking PostGIS behavior).
Fiona fiona.open() infra/ingestion.py Metadata peaking to avoid OOM crashes on large GeoJSONs.
Psycopg2 cursor.execute() infra/database.py Low-level execution of the SQL Spatial Adapters.
SQLAlchemy create_engine() infra/database.py Connection pooling and standardized table synthesis.
Scikit-Learn LinearRegression() core/telemetry.py Fitting machine-specific Log-Log complexity models.
Plotly graph_objects, express core/academic_maps.py Generation of interactive, sigma-filtered analytical maps.

II. Utility & Observability Stack

Library Role Usage
Rich Interface Powers terminal dashboard, progress bars, and animations.
Psutil Telemetry Real-time monitoring of RAM footprint and multi-core utilization.
Mapclassify Statistics Binning of PUI metrics into the Emerald Z-Score scale.
Python-Dotenv Config Management of DB credentials and machine-specific H3 resolutions.
